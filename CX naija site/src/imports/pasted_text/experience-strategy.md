PART II — EXPERIENCE STRATEGY
1. Product Experience Philosophy
The Customer Experience Nigeria website should not feel like a traditional organizational website. It should feel like stepping into an active, thriving professional community.
Every page should answer one or more of these questions within seconds:
What is this community?
Why should I join?
Can I trust them?
What can I learn here?
Who is already part of this?
How do I get involved?
The experience should communicate momentum. Visitors should feel that the community is active, welcoming, and growing.

2. Experience Goals
The website should achieve five emotional outcomes:
Belonging
Visitors should feel that they have found "their people."
Credibility
Organizations should immediately recognize the community as a trusted professional network.
Inspiration
Members should see clear examples of career growth, learning, and impact.
Discovery
Content and resources should encourage exploration rather than one-off visits.
Action
Every page should naturally lead users to a meaningful next step—joining, subscribing, registering for an event, downloading a resource, or starting a partnership conversation.

6. Conversion Strategy
The website should avoid having only one conversion point.
Instead, every page should encourage a meaningful next action.
Member Journey
Visitor → Homepage → Join Community → Welcome Email → Community Platform
Recruiter Journey
Homepage → Hire Talent → Submit Hiring Request → Partnership Follow-up
Sponsor Journey
Homepage → Partner With Us → Sponsorship Information → Partnership Call
Learner Journey
Google Search → Blog Article → Resource Download → Newsletter → Community Membership
This creates multiple entry points into the community ecosystem.

7. Trust Strategy

Use:
