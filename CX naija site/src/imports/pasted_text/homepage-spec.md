Homepage Acceptance Criteria
The homepage is considered complete when:
Loads in under three seconds on a standard mobile connection.
Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
All CTAs route to the correct destinations.
Metrics can be updated through the CMS.
Testimonials and spotlights are CMS-managed.
Event section syncs automatically with Lu.ma or the chosen events platform.
Images are optimized and lazy-loaded.
SEO metadata is implemented.
Accessibility meets WCAG 2.1 AA standards.
Core Web Vitals meet Google's recommended thresholds.

UX & Motion Guidelines

Smooth fade-in animations as sections enter the viewport.
Animated counters for community metrics.
Gentle card lift on hover.

Respect users who prefer reduced motion.
