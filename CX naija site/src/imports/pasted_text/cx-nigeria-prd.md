Executive Summary
Project Overview
Customer Experience Nigeria (CX Nigeria) is the leading community for Customer Experience, Customer Success, Customer Support, Service Design, and Customer-Centric professionals across Nigeria.
The community exists to help professionals connect, learn, grow their careers, share opportunities, and shape the future of Customer Experience across the country.
As the community has grown, its digital presence must evolve beyond social platforms and messaging groups into a centralized, discoverable, and scalable digital home.
The Customer Experience Nigeria website will become the primary destination where professionals discover the community, organizations recruit exceptional talent, partners explore collaboration opportunities, members access valuable resources, and the broader ecosystem recognizes the impact of the community.
The website should not feel like a corporate brochure. It should feel alive—showcasing conversations, events, knowledge sharing, career growth, and community achievements. It should inspire visitors to join, engage, contribute, and advocate for the community.

Product Vision
To build the definitive digital home for Customer Experience professionals in Nigeria—a platform that enables career growth, fosters meaningful connections, promotes knowledge sharing, and serves as the first choice for organizations seeking talent, partnerships, and industry engagement.
Long-term, the website should position Customer Experience Nigeria as one of the most influential professional communities in Africa.

Business Objectives
The website is a strategic growth asset, not just a marketing website.
Its objectives are to:
Increase community membership through a clear and engaging onboarding experience.
Position Customer Experience Nigeria as the leading authority on Customer Experience in Nigeria.
Attract organizations seeking CX talent.
Generate sponsorship and partnership opportunities.
Build long-term organic traffic through educational content and SEO.
Showcase the impact, expertise, and success stories of the community.
Create a scalable platform that grows alongside the community.

Success Metrics (KPIs)
The success of the website will be measured using the following indicators:
Membership Growth
30% increase in community applications within six months.
25% increase in monthly website visitors converting to community members.
Partnerships
20 qualified partnership inquiries per quarter.
10 recruitment inquiries per quarter.
5 sponsorship conversations generated after major events.
Engagement
Average session duration greater than three minutes.
Bounce rate below 45%.
At least three pages viewed per session.
25% returning visitor rate.
Content Performance
500 newsletter subscribers in the first three months.
1,000 resource downloads per quarter.
Increasing organic traffic month over month.

Rank on the first page for:

Product Principles
Every design and product decision should align with the following principles:
Community First
Members are at the center of every experience.
Simplicity
Information should be easy to find, understand, and act upon.
Trust
The website should immediately establish credibility through transparency, impact, and social proof.
Accessibility
The experience should be usable across devices and accessible to people with diverse abilities.
Growth
Every page should support community growth through education, engagement, or conversion.

Brand Personality
The website should reflect the personality of the community.
Professional
High-quality, polished, and credible.
Warm
Friendly and welcoming without feeling informal.
Community-Centered
Real people, real stories, real experiences.
Modern
Clean layouts, thoughtful interactions, and contemporary typography.
Ambitious
Reflecting the vision of growing the Customer Experience profession in Nigeria and across Africa.

Brand Promise
Customer Experience Nigeria helps professionals grow their careers, expand their networks, and shape the future of Customer Experience together.

Positioning Statement
For professionals, Customer Experience Nigeria is the community that accelerates careers through networking, learning, mentorship, and opportunities.
For organizations, it is the most trusted network for discovering, hiring, and engaging Customer Experience talent.
For partners, it is a platform to create meaningful impact within one of Africa’s fastest-growing professional ecosystems.
